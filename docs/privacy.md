# Privacy: CallScribe

This document records the privacy posture for CallScribe. It is produced during the project setup pass and updated whenever the analytics or data collection approach changes.

## Analytics

None. The setup page has no analytics, cookies, or local storage.

## Data collection statement

The current setup page collects, processes, and stores no personal data.

The planned product is expected to handle highly sensitive call audio and transcripts. Its data flows, retention policy, and any optional third-party processing must be agreed and documented before product development begins.

## Third-party services

GitHub Pages serves the public setup page. It receives ordinary web-server request data needed to deliver the page. The page has no embedded third-party services.

## UK GDPR obligations

The current setup page does not itself process personal data. UK GDPR assessment is required before the product processes call audio, transcripts, account data, or any other personal data.

### Lawful basis

For each category of personal data processed, record the lawful basis under UK GDPR Article 6 (for example: consent, legitimate interests, contract performance). Processing personal data without a lawful basis is unlawful.

### Retention periods

Record how long each category of personal data is kept, and why. UK GDPR Article 5(1)(e) requires that data is kept no longer than necessary for the purpose it was collected. State the retention limit and the process for deletion or anonymisation once that limit is reached.

### Data subject rights

Record how this project handles requests from individuals exercising their rights under UK GDPR Articles 15 to 22:

- Article 15: right of access (subject access request).
- Article 16: right to rectification.
- Article 17: right to erasure (right to be forgotten).
- Article 18: right to restriction of processing.
- Article 20: right to data portability.
- Article 21: right to object.

State the process for receiving and responding to each type of request, and the contact route for data subjects.
