Privacy

This application is designed to process speech and screenshots locally in your browser.

Your data stays on your device

Your audio and your transcripts never leave your device. Microphone audio, computer audio, transcripts, screenshots, OCR text, generated meeting notes, model choices and settings are processed and stored only in this browser, on your own computer. The application has no server that receives them and does not send speech, transcript or screenshot content to any cloud service or AI service.

Model downloads

The speech, speaker and meeting-notes models are large files. The first time you use a model, your browser downloads it once and keeps it in browser storage. After that it is reused, including offline where the browser allows. These downloads contact only the following hosts:

  • Hugging Face (huggingface.co), which provides the models. For large files it redirects the browser to its own content delivery hosts, whose names end in hf.co.
  • GitHub (raw.githubusercontent.com), which provides the program files that run the larger meeting-notes models.

The speech recognition runtime and the OCR files come from this site itself. jsDelivr is no longer used. Each download request necessarily shows normal network details, such as your IP address, to the host. The requests never contain audio, transcript text or screenshots. The app is built to contact only the hosts listed here, and automated tests check this. The 2.8 GB Qwen3 4B model needs a separate confirmation before it downloads.

Browser storage and deletion

Downloaded models, saved sessions, screenshots and generated notes remain in this browser until you remove them in the app or clear browser site data. Browser storage is scoped to this site and browser profile; it is not a backup service. Export any material you need to keep.

When data leaves the browser

Your content leaves the browser only when you explicitly download an export or screenshot, or when you use browser-provided sharing features. The site does not use analytics, advertising, tracking cookies, accounts or social-media integrations.

Who is responsible for the data

In most cases you are. The data you record or import is created and kept on your device, and the developer, Tim Dixon, never receives or holds it. You decide what to record, how long to keep it and where to share it. The site is served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs such as IP addresses. The developer does not add tracking to the site.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have rights over personal data about you, including to see it, correct it and delete it. Because this application keeps your data only on your device, you already have full control. To see or copy your data, use the export options. To correct it, edit the transcript or notes. To delete it, use the delete buttons in the app or clear this site's data in your browser. If you think personal data has been handled badly, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office.

Recording other people

A call or meeting usually includes other people, and what they say is their personal data too. Laws and workplace rules on recording differ between countries and organisations. Tell the other people that you are recording and get their consent where the law or your organisation requires it. Keep transcripts only as long as you need them, and delete them when you do not. Do not share a transcript with people who should not see it.

Contact

For questions or feedback, use Contact / feedback. Do not include a transcript or other sensitive material unless you choose to share it.
