Terms and responsible use

Use responsibly

You are responsible for obtaining any consent required to record people, capture audio, record a meeting, or save a shared screen. Laws, workplace rules and meeting policies vary by location and circumstance.

Accuracy and availability

Speech recognition, OCR and screen-change detection can be wrong, incomplete or unavailable. Review important information against the original source. Do not use this application as the only record for legal, medical, financial, safety-critical or emergency decisions.

Your device and data

You are responsible for securing the browser profile and device where your saved sessions are held. Browser storage can be cleared by you, the browser, profile policy or storage pressure. Keep exports where you need a durable copy.

The source code is available on GitHub. Unless a repository licence says otherwise, the software is provided as-is, without warranty.
