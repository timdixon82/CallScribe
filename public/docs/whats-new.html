What’s new in CallScribe

Version 0.6.0 — A simpler workspace and Summary

Released 14 November 2026.

  • Controls and Settings tabs keep the left pane compact. Quick microphone selection and screen sharing sit above recording controls.
  • Settings categories open in the larger right pane. The shared-screen preview stays expanded, without browser stream identifiers.
  • The Summary tab combines options, generation progress, editing, evidence and exports. Generate Summary starts immediately with saved options; Stop generation lets you stop, change options and try again.
  • Completed summaries appear in the Summary tab. Evidence links return to and focus the supporting transcript passage.
  • Mic and App speaker labels now show their distinct audio sources.

Version 0.5.0 — Transcript exports

Released 13 November 2026.

  • Markdown with embedded screenshots is now the default transcript export.
  • Microsoft Word with screenshots (.docx) is available in Export transcript. It includes timestamps, sources, speaker labels and screenshot alternative text.
  • Both formats export the full transcript, including entries hidden by search or filters. Your original transcript stays in this browser.

The footer now links to this page and directly to the full changelog.

Recent improvements in versions 0.4.0 to 0.4.2

  • Capture controls and source status appear beside the transcript on wider screens and stack above it on smaller screens.
  • Microphone and app-audio status distinguish connected sources, excluded microphone text, blocked permissions and sharing without audio.
  • The status summary provides microphone settings, direct screen sharing and screenshot setup shortcuts that move keyboard focus to the relevant controls.
  • Primary controls have visible labels. Space retains its normal behaviour on focused buttons and disclosures.
  • Advanced options and the shared-screen preview remain available through disclosures. Screenshot capture pauses with transcription.

Local meeting notes in version 0.3.0

Generate optional meeting notes locally from a saved transcript, review and edit them, then export them separately. Notes never replace the original transcript.
